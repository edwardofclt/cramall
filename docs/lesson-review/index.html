Cram All · staged for review

Grade 4 Lesson Content

Every South Carolina Grade 4 indicator in Math, Reading, and Science, covered by a full-year lesson catalog. Text content only — visuals and interactive exercises are deliberately not authored in this pass.

What is staged here

82 newly authored lessons across 31 units, completing a 89-lesson catalog that covers all 67 indicators. The 7 previously accepted lessons are listed for context but are not restaged.

Each new lesson carries intro dialogue, three learn cards with inline checks, a worked example, and a 13-question Quick Check with answer key and explanations. Lesson ids, titles, indicators, card titles, concept tags, and question-type mappings follow the frozen Plan C blueprints. Where a blueprint assigns an interactive widget, the card notes it as deferred and teaches fully in text.
